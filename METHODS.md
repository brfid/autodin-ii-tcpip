# Research methods

## Evidence

Keep requirements, proposals, adopted decisions, specifications, static code, reported tests, measured results and deployment distinct. Agreement between a recommendation and a later specification does not recover the intervening decision. A participant retrospective is not independent contemporary corroboration. Preserve contrary evidence beside the finding it qualifies.

Use OCR for discovery. Inspect the decisive scanned passage and enough context to test the strongest supported alternative. Record illegibility, clipped lines, blank leaves, duplicates and uncertain documentary boundaries. Do not treat a catalogue, disposition schedule or failed retrieval as proof of contents, custody, survival or absence.

## Source records

Record title, authorship, historical date and edition, institutional collection and accession where known, retrieval date and full URL, printed/PDF page mappings, exact visual coverage, OCR-only coverage, and unresolved identity. Credit authorship, archival custody and digitization separately; identify an unknown physical locator or digitizer as unknown.

Link to a complete online copy only after checking its identity, edition and cited pages. An abstract, catalogue entry, quotation or landing page is not an equivalent original. Retain a dated failed route as an access limitation. Do not infer uniqueness solely from a failed request. The source catalogue distinguishes complete files from selected-page holdings.

For retained scans, record the original and distributed fingerprints, transformations and page coverage. Remove personal paths and identifying file metadata from distributed derivatives; preserve historical document content and orientation. Keep retained binary scans in Git LFS. Large unselected collections remain outside this repository.

## Continuing the work

Choose one historical question, a named original, a credible route and an exact reading boundary. Consult existing retrieval and coverage records first. A new reading should have a plausible result that could confirm, qualify or contradict the current account. Do not repeat broad searches merely because a new session or agent is available. Contacting an archive, paid access and executing historical software require their own authorization.

At a stopping point, update the affected finding and source record. An unfinished handoff states the document and exact pages reached, the unresolved issue, any access or identity obstacle, and the next bounded action. Update open questions only when priorities change. Use Git for completed work and superseded decisions. A successor should be able to continue from these files without the preceding conversation.
