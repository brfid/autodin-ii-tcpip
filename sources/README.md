## Retained scans and native text

The files under [unique sources](unique/) are selected holdings for which the recorded bounded checks did not authenticate an equivalent complete online copy. This is an access/identity judgment, not a claim that no copy exists elsewhere. Retained binary scans use Git LFS; native text remains directly readable in Git. Photographs are selected pages, not complete reports; the notebooks and reading records retain printed locators and missing portions.

The three PDF packets are IEN 11 (16 pages, annotated paper and appended correspondence), IEN 70 (eight meeting-note pages), and BBN 3900 (75 pages). Document/page metadata was removed in these distributed derivatives. Identifying EXIF, XMP, IPTC and comment metadata was removed from image derivatives while preserving displayed orientation and historical content. Ten DDN Plan photographs and one SRI contract leaf have opaque lower-corner redactions removing the photographer’s hand; these are lossless PNG derivatives with unchanged pixels outside the recorded redaction. Other image derivatives retain compressed image data and decoded pixels. Unknown physical locators and digitizers remain unknown; the scans do not establish present archival custody.

## Source-access limits

Older failed DTIC, GAO, DoD OIG, IDA/NTIS and catalogue requests remain dated observations. A block page or landing page is not a readable original. Access failures do not establish permanent unavailability or uniqueness. An unrecovered evaluation, signed modification, cost analysis or guide remains unrecovered even when its citation is known.

### Archival accession leads

The RG 330 / UD-13W accession leads in the DDN notebook come from a retained accession-metadata transcription. The underlying workbook is not distributed or independently authenticated here. Treat the identifiers as custody leads, not proof of current holdings. The notebooks preserve the separate RG 371 series and the distinction between disposition authority and an actual holdings inventory.
